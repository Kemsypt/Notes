Help
  • What this is: Log your day by entering your Shift start, Lunch start, Lunch end, and Shift end.
  • Now buttons: On today only, each time field has a Now button to stamp the current time (minute accurate). You can still manually edit any day.
  • Views:
    • Daily: shows just today (if it’s Saturday or Sunday, it shows the closest Monday).
    • Weekly: shows the current work week (Monday to Friday).
    • 2-weekly / 4-weekly: shows 2 or 4 work weeks grouped into Week 1, Week 2, etc.
    The view mode can be changed at any time in Settings.
  • Totals: Each day shows Total work, Total lunch, and Flex next to the date.
  • Notes: Use the Note button to attach a note to that day. Notes appear in the Summary.
  • Summary: Use the Summary button when you need to copy or review your hours for entry into TMS. It shows every day in the 4‑week period with all times and notes.
  • Saving & backup: The form saves automatically to your browser cache (local storage). If you ever need to clear your browser cache, use Settings → Export CSV first, then Import CSV to restore your data.
